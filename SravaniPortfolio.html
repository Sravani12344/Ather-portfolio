Design and innovation

I don’t have professional design experience yet. This is how I think about the work and what I’m learning.

Clear interfaces

A good screen tells people what to do next. I try to keep forms, statuses, and labels simple enough that a first-time user understands them.

Start with the problem

CivicPulse began with a daily frustration: citizens don’t know what happened to their complaint. Tracking and status were the first things I wanted to get right.

Technology that lasts

AquaPulse comes from an interest in sustainability: knowing when a filter really needs changing could reduce waste and make maintenance less guesswork.
