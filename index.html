03 / AI Workflow Concept

DesignFlow — a concept for smoother design work

Proposed concept, not a completed application: explore how a simple AI-assisted tool could help designers organize feedback and reduce repetitive documentation.

The problem

Designers may receive feedback across multiple notes and messages. Organizing it manually can take time and make it harder to identify recurring issues.

Proposed workflow

01 Collect feedback
02 Organize themes
03 Review AI suggestions
04 Export a summary

How I would evaluate it

  • Compare time spent organizing feedback before and after.
  • Check whether categories are accurate and useful.
  • Ask designers whether the workflow is easy to use.

This is a proposed idea. Do not present it as an implemented AI integration unless you build and test it.
